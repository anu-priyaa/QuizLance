About QuizLance

QuizLance is a smart online quiz and classroom platform designed to simplify assessments and enhance learning experiences. It enables teachers to create quizzes, manage classes, and analyze student performance, while students can attempt quizzes and track their progress.

How QuizLance Works

For Teachers

Teachers can create classes, design quizzes, schedule assessments, and evaluate student performance using analytics.

For Students

Students can join classes using a code, attend quizzes, view instant results, and track academic progress.
